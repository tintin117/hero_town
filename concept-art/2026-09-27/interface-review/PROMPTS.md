Central large arena occupies about 65% width and most playable world height: broad sand oval, curved two-row timber spectator stands around BACK and SIDES, low stone front wall that does not occlude fighters, modest blue-and-cream canopy, heraldic bunting. Clear raised entrance on right and a visible ticket queue. Arena brightest, background less saturated. Left edge: small blue-roof tavern, smoking chimney, two tables, tiny strolling visitors. Right edge: modest armorer, archery targets and grass expansion plot; buildings are scenery placeholders without new resource mechanics or price buttons. Three compact selected hero chips with expressive small portraits and names+levels (Bram Lv5, Ivo Lv6, Nia Lv10), then two smaller reserve portraits Tuck and Rook. No huge five-column stat cards; detailed stats are intentionally hidden. Two labeled secondary buttons "Heroes" and "Arena". A concise earnings block "238 at finish" with small "60 tips paid", and a clear amber primary control "Stop after fight". The UI does not cover combat. Give the composition professional hierarchy and generous margins. No oversized title or long instructional copy.
